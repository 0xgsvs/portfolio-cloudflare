// FIRST IMPRESSION

White, pastel, and loud in a friendly way. The name lands first inside a yellow highlight, then the eye slides to the centered tagline, the curl box, and down one centered column of equal cards. What I remember: the highlight, the drop-shadow offset, and that everything is centered. What I do not remember: a single project, a single claim, or a reason to scroll. The strongest idea on the site, that this person thinks in a terminal, is not in the first viewport.

The material is worth keeping. The composition, the type system, and the access layer are not.

HIGH / ACCESSIBILITY + MOTION

Four equal-weight tile grids in one page

index.astro:42–61, 73–99, 108–114, 152–172. Projects, dev toolkit, tech stack, and contact all render as uniform grids. Three projects of clearly unequal importance are presented as peers.
